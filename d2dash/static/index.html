Drive starting
Engine data paused: SLABS is the active module. Reconnecting takes a few seconds.
The K-line carries one ECU at a time: SLABS is active, so engine data is paused. Reconnecting takes a few seconds; the ABS lights go out as it does.

Injector balance

Fuel correction per cylinder. Healthy injectors sit near zero and stay put; one that drifts away from the others is tired.
